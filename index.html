A little context / 03

ABOUT ME.

Arnav Karmakar

Filmmaker / Photographer / India

I am Arnav Karmakar, a filmmaker and photographer based in India. My work is guided by an interest in people, places and the stories that emerge when you take the time to observe.

I am especially drawn to documentary filmmaking and travel stories that feel personal, grounded and honest. I like natural moments, thoughtful framing and the details that give a place its character.

For me, filmmaking and photography are connected practices. One unfolds through movement, sound and time; the other makes a single moment stay. Both are ways of understanding the world around me.

I am open to independent projects, collaborations and opportunities to work with people who care about the stories they tell.
