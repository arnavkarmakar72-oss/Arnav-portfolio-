02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Paying
attention is
the start.

I’m Arnav — a filmmaker and photographer drawn to stories that unfold when you slow down and look closer.

I’m especially interested in documentary and travel filmmaking: the people behind a place, the rhythms of everyday life, and the small details that stay with you long after a journey ends.

My approach is observational and collaborative. I care about honest moments, thoughtful frames, natural sound and stories that don't need to shout to be heard.
