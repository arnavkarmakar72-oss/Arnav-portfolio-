02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

LOOKING
CLOSER.

I am Arnav Karmakar, a filmmaker and photographer interested in the relationship between people and the places they inhabit.

I’m especially interested in documentary and travel filmmaking: the people behind a place, the rhythms of everyday life, and the small details that stay with you long after a journey ends.

I enjoy observing, listening and finding stories in the details of everyday life. Film lets me spend time with a story; photography lets me hold on to a moment. Both begin with the same curiosity: looking a little closer.
